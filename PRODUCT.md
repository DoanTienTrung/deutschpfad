# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Vietnamese working adults preparing to study or work in Germany, studying toward Goethe/telc/TestDaF certification. They fit study into short, frequent sessions around a job — often on a phone, sometimes at a desk — and need the app to work well for quick daily review as much as focused study blocks.

## Product Purpose

DeutschPfad is a German-learning and exam-prep platform: vocabulary with spaced repetition, listening/speaking, reading/writing, grammar, and a full exam-simulation engine with AI grading, building toward Goethe/telc/TestDaF certification. Success is the learner reaching the certification level they need for their visa, job, or study application.

## Positioning

A free, low-cost, structured path to German certification for Vietnamese learners heading to Germany — a guided roadmap in place of scattered free resources or expensive centers.

## Brand Personality

Warm, friendly, motivating. The tone should feel like a supportive study companion, not a bureaucratic test-prep portal — softening the pressure of exam deadlines rather than adding to it. Encouraging copy, approachable visuals, momentum-building feedback (streaks, progress) over cold, clinical presentation.

## Anti-references

No specific anti-references named yet; avoid cold/bureaucratic test-portal aesthetics and Duolingo-style casual-fluency framing (this is exam-certification-focused, not general chit-chat practice).

## Design Principles

- Exam-focused, not general fluency: every surface should reinforce progress toward a certification level (A1–C1), not just casual practice.
- Built for short, frequent sessions: prioritize fast entry into "what do I review today," low friction on mobile, quick resumability.
- Warmth over bureaucracy: encouraging, human tone in copy and feedback, even when the underlying task (exam prep) is high-stakes.
- Progress made visible: streaks, SRS review counts, and level progress should be legible at a glance to sustain motivation.
- Affordable and accessible by design: the product's edge is being a free/low-cost structured alternative — visuals and flows shouldn't feel gated or premium-only.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Baseline best practice: WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` support. No additional requirements specified beyond solid defaults.
